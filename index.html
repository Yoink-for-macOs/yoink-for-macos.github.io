Yoink for Mac

Yoink simplifies drag-and-drop across macOS Spaces and fullscreen apps. It fades in a dedicated window on your screen's edge, letting you park files mid-drag, navigate freely, and resume seamlessly.

Effortless Multi-Space Dragging

Start a drag, drop into Yoink's window, switch Spaces or apps without losing your files. Retrieve them instantly when ready. No more awkward mouse gymnastics.

Enhanced Quick Look

Improved sizing for diverse file types. Pin the Quick Look panel to keep it visible across app switches and Spaces—preview without interruption.

Reliable Clipboard History

Capture and access recent clips with a contextual menu. Renaming works flawlessly for web images, text snippets, and more.

Stable and Polished

Optimized for macOS 26 Tahoe: menu icons respect system settings. Fixed wake-from-sleep positioning, input recognition, and rare hangs for uninterrupted workflow.

 

How It Works

1. Start Dragging

Grab any file, image, or text. Yoink's window appears on the screen's left edge.

2. Park and Navigate

Drop your item into Yoink. Free your mouse to switch Spaces, apps, or fullscreen windows.

3. Resume and Drop

Return to Yoink, drag out your item, and drop it exactly where needed.

4. Quick Look On Demand

Hover for previews. Pin panels for persistent viewing across your workflow.

 

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. It bridges Spaces and fullscreen seamlessly, keeping your drags intact.
